Change Readiness Briefing

A structured assessment that helps leaders identify transformation risks, understand organizational readiness, and improve the conditions required for successful change.

Why readiness matters

Organizations often invest significant resources into transformation strategies, technologies, and implementation plans. Yet many initiatives struggle because the organization itself is not prepared for the change ahead.

The Change Readiness Briefing identifies the gap between transformation ambition and organizational reality.

The Six Dimensions of Readiness

The assessment evaluates six critical areas that influence transformation success.

1. Clarity of Purpose

Do stakeholders understand why the change is happening, what success looks like, and how it supports organizational objectives?

2. Stakeholder Alignment

Are key stakeholders aligned, engaged, and prepared to support the transformation?

3. Change Culture

Does the organization have the adaptability, mindset, and capacity required to embrace change?

4. Executive Ownership

Are leaders actively sponsoring the transformation and accountable for its success?

5. People Engagement

Are employees informed, involved, and prepared to adopt new ways of working?

6. Delivery Readiness

Are the processes, resources, capabilities, and governance structures in place to execute successfully?

What leaders receive

The Change Readiness Briefing provides a structured view of organizational readiness, including:

  • Key readiness strengths and gaps
  • Potential transformation risks
  • Priority areas requiring attention
  • Practical recommendations to improve readiness
