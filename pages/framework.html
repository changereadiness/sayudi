Our Framework

Transformation programs rarely fail for lack of strategy or technology. They fail because the organizational conditions required to sustain change were never assessed. Sayudi evaluates those conditions using a structured model: the Initiative Stability Index.

The Initiative Stability Index

A framework for evaluating whether a transformation initiative has the organizational conditions required for sustainable success — independent of the strategy, technology, or timeline involved.

Beyond change management

Most change frameworks focus on how to manage change once it's underway. The ISI focuses on a question leaders need answered earlier: is this initiative built to hold under pressure?

A structured, comparable view

The ISI evaluates stability across the same dimensions that determine whether alignment, adoption, and execution actually happen — purpose, sponsorship, culture, and delivery capability.

How the ISI relates to the Change Readiness Briefing

The ISI is the underlying model. The Change Readiness Briefing is how it's delivered to clients.

01. Initiative Stability Index

The framework Sayudi uses to evaluate organizational readiness and initiative stability.

02. Change Readiness Briefing

A structured assessment that applies the ISI to your organization, producing a readiness score, risk areas, and priority actions specific to your transformation.

Think of the ISI as the instrument, and the Change Readiness Briefing as the reading it produces for your organization.
