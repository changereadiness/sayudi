The Sayudi Readiness Framework

A structured approach to understanding whether an organization is prepared to adopt, sustain, and realize value from transformational change.

Why Readiness Matters

Transformation initiatives often focus on strategy, technology, and execution plans while underestimating the organizational conditions required for adoption and sustained success.

The Sayudi Readiness Framework helps leaders identify potential barriers, strengthen alignment, and create the conditions required for successful transformation.

The Sayudi Readiness Framework

Transformation readiness depends on six organizational conditions that influence alignment, adoption, and execution.

01

Clarity of Purpose

Is the reason for change clearly understood, communicated, and connected to organizational objectives?

02

Stakeholder Alignment

Are leaders, sponsors, and key stakeholders aligned on priorities, expectations, and desired outcomes?

03

Executive Ownership

Are leaders actively sponsoring the transformation and accountable for creating the conditions for success?

04

Change Culture

Does the organization have the mindset, adaptability, and capacity required to navigate change?

05

People Engagement

Are employees informed, involved, and prepared to adopt new ways of working?

06

Delivery Readiness

Are capabilities, resources, governance, and execution structures in place to support successful implementation?
