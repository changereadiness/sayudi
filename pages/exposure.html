EXPOSURE INDEX

See what the dashboard cannot.

Transformation exposure is the distance between what leaders believe is happening and what the organization is actually capable of sustaining.

The Exposure Index examines six organizational vectors that can remain invisible while a transformation appears healthy.

01
Purpose
Does the organization share the same understanding of why the transformation exists?
Meaning can fragment long before strategy does.
02
Interests
What incentives, interests, power, or losses may influence how people respond?
Organizational behaviour is rarely independent of what people stand to gain or lose.
03
Adaptability
Can the organization change course when reality invalidates the original plan?
A transformation that cannot adapt can continue executing the wrong answer with impressive discipline.
04
Ambassadors
Are the leaders closest to implementation transmitting the transformation as intended?
Executive intent changes as it travels through the organization.
05
Adoption
Are people actually changing behaviour, or merely complying with the new requirements?
Visible compliance can conceal a complete absence of behavioural adoption.
06
Execution
Can the organization actually deliver what the transformation now requires?
Execution exposes the distance between the plan and organizational reality.
HOW IT WORKS

Evaluate your own exposure and risks. Coming soon.

The Exposure Index is designed to move below the level of conventional transformation reporting. It does not ask whether the project is on track. It asks what risk conditions exist underneath that assessment.

Each vector is examined through targeted questions designed to surface inconsistencies, hidden dependencies, competing interests, weak signals, and conditions that conventional reporting can miss.

THE QUESTIONS

Exposure becomes visible through better questions.

The Index is not intended to produce another score that obscures the underlying issue. The questions are designed to make the issue harder to ignore.

PURPOSE

Is the transformation still solving the same problem?

Strategic language can remain unchanged while the organization's understanding of the problem shifts.

INTERESTS

Who has something to lose?

Resistance often makes more sense once the underlying incentives and interests are visible.

ADAPTABILITY

What happens when the plan stops making sense?

The ability to recognize and respond to changing conditions is itself a transformation capability.

AMBASSADORS

What is actually being transmitted?

Messages can change between the executive room and the people responsible for implementation.

ADOPTION

What remains when nobody is watching?

Sustainable adoption appears in behaviour after supervision, campaigns, and formal pressure recede.

EXECUTION

Can the organization deliver what it now knows?

The ability to execute is constrained by organizational capacity, not simply by the quality of the plan.

SEE DIFFERENTLY

The exposure is already there.

The question is whether you can see it before it becomes a problem.
