About Sayudi

A founder-led transformation advisory firm helping organizations build readiness, strengthen alignment, and navigate complex change with confidence.

Our Perspective

Transformation goes beyond being a strategic or technological challenge. It is an organizational challenge. Successful transformation requires alignment between leadership intent, organizational reality, and the ability of people to adopt new ways of working.

Founder & Principal Advisor

Sayudi is led by Ali Saïd, a transformation advisor with experience spanning enterprise technology, digital transformation, organizational change, and AI industry partnerships.

Through work across technology adoption and organizational transformation initiatives, Ali helps leaders understand readiness challenges, align stakeholders, and create the conditions required for sustainable change.

Experience Across Transformation

Sayudi brings together perspectives from enterprise technology, organizational change, and emerging technologies to help leaders navigate complex transformation.

Enterprise Technology

Experience supporting technology adoption and understanding how new capabilities reshape processes, operating models, and employee experiences.

Organizational Change

Expertise in readiness assessment, stakeholder alignment, and adoption strategies that help organizations successfully navigate change.

AI & Emerging Technologies

A focus on helping organizations evaluate and adopt emerging technologies by balancing opportunity, risk, and organizational readiness.
