About Sayudi

A founder-led advisory firm helping organizations reduce transformation risk through readiness, alignment, and practical change expertise.

Our Perspective

Transformation is not only a strategic or technological challenge. It is an organizational challenge.

Sayudi was built on the belief that successful transformation requires alignment between leadership intent, organizational reality, and the ability of people to adopt new ways of working.

Experience Across Transformation

Sayudi's approach is shaped by experience supporting organizations through enterprise technology adoption, digital transformation, organizational change, and emerging technology initiatives.

By combining perspectives from technology, business transformation, and change management, Sayudi helps leaders identify risks, improve readiness, and create the conditions required for sustainable change.

Enterprise Technology

Experience supporting technology adoption and helping organizations translate emerging capabilities into business value.

Change Leadership

Expertise in readiness assessment, stakeholder alignment, adoption strategy, and organizational transformation.

AI & Emerging Technologies

Supporting organizations as they evaluate and integrate new technologies responsibly and effectively.

Founder & Principal Advisor

Sayudi is led by Ali Saïd, a change management practitioner with experience across enterprise technology, digital transformation, and AI industry partnerships.
