About Sayudi

A founder-led transformation advisory firm helping organizations build readiness, strengthen alignment, and navigate complex change with confidence.

Our Perspective

Transformation is not only a strategic or technological challenge. It is an organizational challenge.

Successful transformation requires alignment between leadership intent, organizational reality, and the ability of people to adopt new ways of working.

Founder & Principal Advisor

Through work across technology adoption and organizational transformation initiatives, Ali helps leaders understand readiness challenges, align stakeholders, and create the conditions required for sustainable change.
