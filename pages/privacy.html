Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 5, 2026

Sayudi Inc. ("Sayudi", "we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide through our website and business interactions.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal information collected through www.sayudi.com and related services.

Information We Collect

We may collect personal information when you interact with our website, request information, schedule a consultation, or engage with our services.

Information collected may include:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Organization name
  • Professional role or title
  • Telephone number, if provided
  • Information submitted through contact forms or email correspondence
  • Website usage information such as browser type, device information, and pages visited

How We Use Information

We use collected information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries and requests
  • Communicating with prospective and existing clients
  • Scheduling consultations, workshops, and advisory engagements
  • Providing and improving our services
  • Maintaining business records
  • Meeting legal and regulatory requirements

Payment Processing

Certain services may be purchased through secure payment links provided by Square. Payment transactions are processed directly by Square and are subject to Square's own privacy policy and terms of service.

Sayudi does not collect, process, or store payment card information.

Cookies and Website Analytics

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to improve functionality and understand website usage.

Analytics tools may collect anonymous information such as visitor traffic patterns, device information, and website interactions. This information is used to improve website performance and user experience.

Information Sharing

Sayudi does not sell, rent, or trade personal information.

We may share information with trusted service providers that support our operations, including website hosting, payment processing, communication, and technology services. These providers are authorized to use personal information only as necessary to provide their services.

We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to protect our legal rights, security, or business interests.

Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data Security

Sayudi applies reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Third-Party Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including professional networks, payment providers, and other external resources.

Sayudi is not responsible for the privacy practices, security, or content of third-party websites. We encourage users to review the privacy policies of any external websites they visit.

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable privacy legislation, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including:

  • Requesting access to personal information we hold about you
  • Requesting corrections to inaccurate information
  • Requesting deletion of personal information where appropriate
  • Withdrawing consent where processing is based on consent
  • Requesting information about our privacy practices

To exercise these rights, please contact us using the information below.

Children's Privacy

Sayudi's website and services are intended for professional and business audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

Sayudi may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in our practices, services, or legal obligations.

Any updates will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
